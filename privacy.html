2. In short

  • We only process what the game needs. We do not sell data and only share it for advertising if you have agreed to this.

3. What data we process and why

We collect data automatically through the game: from what you enter, from gameplay and from the technical connection.

  • Account: email address, password (only as an encrypted hash at Google Firebase – we never see it), with "Sign in with Google/Apple" the ID and email address of that account, a random account ID. Purpose: signing in and protecting your account. Legal basis: contract (KVKK Art. 5(2)(c); GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Verification code: When you register, we send a 6-digit code to your email address. We only store a hash of the code, its expiry time and the number of attempts – for at most one day. If a code email cannot be sent, Google Firebase sends a verification link instead. Purpose: real addresses, protection against mass accounts. Legal basis: contract and legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5(2)(f); GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Profile (visible to others): player name, colour, creation date, game statistics (games, wins), level, equipped items, player tag (e.g. #ABC234). Other players can retrieve your profile and name; your name appears in rankings and World League lists. With "Profile is private", opponents in a match only see your level instead of your statistics and rating. Legal basis: contract.
  • Game data: moves, game state, result, match duration, rating (ELO), XP, level, coins with transaction history, virtual items, login calendar and level rewards. Purpose: running the game, progress, fair rating. To prevent point pushing, we count how often the same two accounts score against each other; these counters expire after two days at the latest. Legal basis: contract; fraud prevention legitimate interest.
  • World League: your chosen country and your points per week and in total, publicly visible with your player name. Your country is also shown as a small flag next to your name in every online match (for your opponent too) and in your profile. For cheat detection we keep a match log (account IDs of both players, opponent's name, countries, time, duration, number of moves, how the match ended); it is deleted after at most 9 weeks. Legal basis: contract; match log legitimate interest.
  • Friends and online status: friend list, friend requests, invitations, block list; your online status (online, last seen, in a match) is visible to signed-in players. Spectators see the game state and names, not the chat. Legal basis: contract.
  • Chat and emotes: messages pass through our game server, are filtered automatically and processed only for delivery. So that you can report messages, the server keeps the last lines of the current match (at most 20) in memory; during a server update they are in the database for a few seconds. They are discarded after the match. Legal basis: contract.
  • Reports, moderation, bans: When something is reported, we store the reporting and the reported account, the reported message, the match chat and the time. For warnings and bans: account ID, reason and duration. Purpose: a safe game, protecting other players, preventing ban evasion. Legal basis: legitimate interest and establishing, exercising or defending rights (KVKK Art. 5(2)(e) and (f); GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Error and problem reports: If a technical error occurs, the game stores a short report (error message, code location, browser/device type, app version, time) under your account ID – at most 10, older ones are overwritten. After an online match you can voluntarily send a problem report (your description, device data, the match history and server data of the match) – at most 3 per day. Purpose: finding and fixing bugs. Legal basis: legitimate interest; for problem reports also your request.
  • Ideas & feedback: If you send us an idea from the options menu, we store your text (filtered automatically, at most 1,000 characters), the time, whether you used the app or the browser, and your language under your account ID. Only our team reads it. Purpose: improving the game. Legal basis: your request and legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5(2)(f); GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Technical data: the game server uses your IP address only in memory for abuse and overload protection (e.g. limits for code emails); it is not stored in our database. The server also sees whether you use the app or the browser. The hosting provider of the game server keeps server logs (including account IDs in error messages), which it deletes after 30 days at the latest. Legal basis: legitimate interest.
  • Anonymous statistics: we only count daily totals – connections, playing time, matches per mode, app or browser and a country derived from your language setting. Without account ID and without IP address; it cannot be traced back to you.
  • Support and gifts: when we change something in your account (e.g. correct coins, gift items or a message, ban), we log what was done, when, to which account and why. Purpose: accountability. Legal basis: legitimate interest.
  • Acceptance of the Terms of Use: when you sign up (or confirm new terms), we store the time, the version of the terms and whether you used the app or the browser, as proof of the contract. Legal basis: contract and legitimate interest in being able to prove it (KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) and (f); GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). Deleted together with your account.
  • Guests: if you play without an account, Google Firebase creates a random anonymous ID without name or email. Guests do not earn coins or XP.
  • Storage on your device: settings (language, sound, appearance), sign-in state, your consent choice, a local history of your replays and the guest ID are kept in your browser's or the app's storage. This is technically necessary for the game; without your consent we do not set tracking or advertising cookies.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile, game data, friends, error and problem reports: until you delete your account.
  • Ideas & feedback: 90 days (deleted the next time we open the list), earlier together with your account.
  • Verification code data: at most 1 day.
  • World League weekly lists and match log: at most 9 weeks.
  • Counters against point abuse between two accounts: at most 2 days.
  • Unprocessed reports: at most 12 months.
  • Ban and moderation history: as long as needed to prevent ban evasion; chat excerpts in it are deleted after 12 months.
  • Support log (changes to accounts): permanently, for accountability.
  • Server logs of the game server: at most 30 days.
  • Delivery logs of the email service Brevo (recipient address, time, delivery status): 1 month.
  • Database backups: 30 days – deleted data disappears from them by then at the latest.

7. Deleting your account

Browser: open your profile → "Delete account". App: Profile → "Danger Zone" → "Delete account". For security you confirm with your password or briefly sign in again with Google or Apple. If you can no longer sign in, write to us from your account's email address. What is deleted and what remains for which reason is described at wrongway.app/delete-account.html.

8. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed and to request information about it; to learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly; to know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred; to have incomplete or incorrect data corrected and data deleted or destroyed, and to request that recipients be notified; to object to a result to your detriment that arises exclusively from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

Under the GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 15–21), and the right to withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future.

How to apply: by email from the address registered in your account to info@hupsy.app, or in writing to the address in section 1. We answer free of charge and within 30 days at the latest.

Complaints: in Turkey to the Personal Data Protection Authority (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu, kvkk.gov.tr) after first contacting us; in the EU to the data protection supervisory authority where you live.

9. Children

The game is for people aged 13 and over. If you are younger, please do not create an account. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we delete it.

10. Security

All connections are encrypted (TLS). Only Google Firebase knows passwords, as a hash. Only our server changes game-deciding data such as rating, coins and items; the database access rules block everything that is not explicitly allowed.

11. Changes

If the processing changes, we update this policy; the date at the top shows the current version. We announce significant changes in the game.

9. Enfants

Le jeu s'adresse aux personnes âgées de 13 ans et plus. Si tu es plus jeune, ne crée pas de compte. Si nous apprenons qu'un compte appartient à un enfant de moins de 13 ans, nous le supprimons.

10. Sécurité

Toutes les connexions sont chiffrées (TLS). Seul Google Firebase connaît les mots de passe, sous forme de hachage. Seul notre serveur modifie les données décisives du jeu comme le classement, les pièces et les objets ; les règles d'accès de la base de données bloquent tout ce qui n'est pas expressément autorisé.
